Common questions


What size of solar system do I need?

It depends on the appliances you run and for how long. Use the calculator above for a rough idea, then request a free assessment.

How long does installation take?

Most home and shop systems take one to a few days. Larger jobs take longer. We confirm the timeline in your quote.

Do you offer warranty?

Yes. Warranty terms depend on the product. We help you with claims after installation.

Can solar run my AC, fridge and freezer?

Yes, with a system sized for those loads. Air conditioners need a larger inverter and battery bank.

How do I maintain my system?

Keep panels clean, keep batteries ventilated and book a periodic check with us.

Do you install outside Makurdi?

Yes, we serve Benue State and beyond. Tell us your location when you request a quote.
